MediBox Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

MediBox is an offline household medicine inventory organizer that requires no account. Medicine details, batches, notes, and package photos stay in the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded to the developer or third-party servers.

Package text recognition uses on-device models included with the app and on-device text recognition provided by Apple. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, or developer-hosted backend.

Notifications are used only for expiry alerts you enable. Camera access is used only on the capture screen. Photos are read only when you select them through Apple's system photo picker.

You can create a password-encrypted local backup and choose where to save it. Neither the app nor the developer stores your password. You can delete records inside the app; removing the app removes its local data. App-private data is excluded from system cloud backups. Delete exported backups and shared copies separately. If you choose iCloud or another file service, that service handles the file under its own policy. Package photos are re-encoded before storage to remove location and other metadata.

The app is only for inventory organization. It does not provide medication reminders, diagnosis, treatment, or dosage advice, and it is not a medical device.
