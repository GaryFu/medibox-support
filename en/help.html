Help & Support

Use the add button on the home screen to start capturing. Photograph the front, the panel containing the batch and expiry date, and the package instructions. You may capture 3–6 photos and open any thumbnail to crop or rotate it.

After recognition, review the medicine name, specifications, production date, batch number, and expiry date. A field marked for review lets you inspect the relevant photo, choose another candidate, or enter the value manually.

Expiry notifications are optional and can be enabled in Settings. Backups use a password of at least eight characters. The password cannot be recovered, so keep it safe.

For help, email youmin2025@icloud.com with the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid including unnecessary health or personal information.

Backups and restore

New .medibox backups work across Android and iOS, including medicines, batches, locations, package photos, and pending reviews. Older .medibox and .mediboxios files cannot be imported. App upgrades preserve on-device data; export a new backup after upgrading.

Import checks the password and file, then shows record counts before you confirm replacement. Notification permission and the main switch stay local. The file limit is 100 MB.

Recognition results

Blur, glare, and embossed text can cause errors. Check dates and batch numbers. Ingredients, indications, and directions are transcribed package information, not medical advice.
